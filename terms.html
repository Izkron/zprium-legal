Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. The service

  • automations;
  • esports tools;
  • local artificial intelligence features.

It is operated by Isaac Dereck Lizana Correa (Dereck Lizana) ("we") on its own hardware. By adding Zprium to a server or using its commands, you accept these terms and the privacy policy.

2. Requirements

  • You must follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, including the minimum age.
  • Whoever adds Zprium to a server confirms they have the authority to do so.

3. Administrators' responsibilities

Each server's administrators decide:

  • which modules to turn on;
  • how to configure them;
  • which permissions to give the bot.

In particular:

  • Aegis "enforce" mode. If an administrator turns it on, Zprium may act on its own in that server, following the actions the administrator chose: quarantining or kicking the account it attributes a burst of destructive actions to, and pausing invites. Every action is recorded and can be reviewed. Turning this mode on is the administrator's decision and responsibility.
  • Giveaways and prize activities: the administrator is responsible for keeping them lawful. Zprium does not allow paid entry.
  • Content the server provides (knowledge base documents, automation texts, sounds): the server confirms it has the right to use it.

You may also not try to:

  • access other servers' data;
